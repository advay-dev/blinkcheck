How the test runs
  1. 1Calibrate, 3 s. The dot starts moving and the app learns how far your irises travel for a given dot position. Nothing is scored yet.
  2. 2Track, 12 s. Eye velocity is differentiated frame by frame and compared with the analytic velocity of the dot.
  3. 3Score. Error is summarised as RMSE, gain, lag and saccade rate.

Stimulus: simple harmonic motion,  Hz, peak target speed  s-1.

Eye vs. target velocity

BlinkCheck is a hackathon prototype, not a medical device. It does not diagnose concussion, intoxication or any condition, and its thresholds are provisional defaults that have not been validated against a clinical reference. Do not use it to clear or ground a driver.

Nothing here is transmitted — everything stays on this device. This just measures how small the active driver's full record actually is, as a stand-in for what a future backend sync would need to send.

A real backend wouldn't re-send the whole profile every update — only what changed. This measures just the pending changes since the last check, then clears that log, the same way a real client would drop a change once its sync confirms delivery.
